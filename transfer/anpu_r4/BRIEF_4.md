# Codex image brief: round 4, Anpu's attendant spirits

References for every image: ../out/emissaries_family.png, ../out2/obsidian_guardians_v2.png, ref_ankhu_sheet.png. Prompts below are exactly what is sent.

## Prompt 1

Reference images: emissaries_family.png sets the shared attendant body and the render style. obsidian_guardians_v2.png shows the polished obsidian material. ref_ankhu_sheet.png sets the render style and plain grey sheet layout only.

Shared base for all attendants: ONE identical lean human body, about 1.85 m, same proportions in every figure, so one 3D body and rig serves them all. Only the head, skin material, kilt colour and one or two flush rigid accessories change. Every figure: pleated shendyt kilt (white or yellow-ochre) stiff and above the knee with a gap to the thighs, a broad beaded usekh collar flush on the chest, bare feet, hands empty. No gold heavier than small accents (gold belongs to Anpu). Heads are rigid sculpted forms with clean necks.

IMAGE: attendants_roster_front. Six attendants, FRONT A-pose, in one row left to right in this order, same scale, feet on one baseline, even gaps:
 a) Sab, jackal escort: jackal head with SHORT muzzle and short ears (clearly smaller than Anpu's), charcoal-grey fur, a red linen band tied flat around the neck, white kilt.
 b) Gate Warden: crocodile head, deep blue-green skin (Osirian green), yellow-ochre kilt, leather wristguards.
 c) Ram Herald: ram head with curled horns tight against the skull, fiery red-orange skin, white kilt, red sash belt.
 d) Serpent Sentinel: cobra head with the hood folded flat and rigid against the neck, jet-black skin with faint blue sheen, white kilt.
 e) He Whose Face Is Behind Him (the Book of the Dead ferryman Hraf-haf): human body facing forward but the head is turned completely backwards, so from the front we see the back of a shaved head, calm and uncanny, blue-green skin, yellow-ochre kilt.
 f) Obsidian Torch-Priest: human head, bald, body and head of polished glassy black obsidian, white calcite eyes with black pupils, round human ears, white kilt.

STYLE: Sculpted Dream style, character model sheet for a stylized 3D game, soft sculpted forms like a hand-crafted miniature at heroic scale, matte painted materials, even neutral studio lighting from the front with soft ambient occlusion, no dramatic glow, no rim light, same rendering style as the reference Ankhu sheet.
LAYOUT: flat plain uniform mid grey background (#7F7F7F), no floor texture, no shadows on the background, every figure full body from top of ears to soles with generous margin, all figures the same size and with feet on one shared baseline, orthographic views, no overlapping figures.
POSE: A-pose, arms straight and angled down about 45 degrees away from the body with a clear gap between arms and torso, hands open with fingers slightly apart, legs straight and apart at hip width with a clear gap between the legs, standing on flat feet, looking straight ahead, mouth closed.
CONSTRUCTION (critical, the image will be converted to a 3D model): every garment edge stands away from the skin, the kilt is stiff starched linen that flares outward like a rigid triangle and ends above the knee with a clear visible gap between kilt hem and both thighs, nothing hangs between the legs, no long ribbons, no tassels, no fringe, no loose straps, no capes or cloaks, no long hair, ears are tall rigid upright triangles, jewellery is solid and flush to the body (broad collar, armbands, wristbands), hands are EMPTY (no staff, no weapon, no object of any kind in any view).
BANNED: text, labels, hieroglyph-like writing, watermark, border, props in hands, cape, crook, flail, long robe, transparent fabric, motion blur, glow, fire, background scenery, extra characters, chibi proportions, bodybuilder anatomy.
